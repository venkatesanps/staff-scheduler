Step one

First, tell it who you have.

Add people whenever you hire them. Give each a role, a cap on working days, and a single tick if they're allowed weekends off. That tick lives in one place, so there's exactly one place to look.

Step two

Then write the rules,
in plain English.

Compose each rule from a small grammar of who, what and when, then mark it hard or soft. The card reads your rule back as a sentence, and that sentence is what shows up in every explanation.

At least 2 Supervisors work on weekends.

Then it builds the fortnight.

One pass, day by day. Required people first, then the rest ranked by who has worked least, then the coverage floors. Weekends fill to their staffing level and the spare weekend off rotates.

Tap any day.
Read why.

Every decision comes with a sentence in your own rule names. Tap a person for their day, or a date header for the whole day: who's on, who's off, and which rules actually acted.
