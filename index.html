Scheduling rules

Rules are listed in the order the scheduler applies them. The numbered tiers are fixed — they are how the engine works — but you can reorder rules within a tier with ↑↓, and that is where order changes the outcome. Hard rules are never broken; soft rules bend when coverage would otherwise fail.
